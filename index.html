At Gurnee Barber Shop, we believe a great haircut is more than just a trim—it's an experience. Located on Old Grand Ave, our shop has built a lasting reputation for dependable, high-quality grooming in a clean, quiet, and friendly environment.

Whether you're coming in for a routine touch-up, a fresh fade, or bringing your child in for a haircut, our skilled and patient barbers (like our long-time customer favorite, Melinda) take the time to deliver exactly what you ask for.
